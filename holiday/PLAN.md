# Marbled LA Holiday 2026 email plan

Store: https://order.marbled.la/holiday (add-to-cart links use `?add=<productId>[,<id>]`).
Generator: `python3 holiday/build.py OUTDIR IMGBASE` writes 15 emails plus `_images.json` (the image crops each needs).

## Rules from Tro
- No holiday emails before Mon 11/30 (Monday after Thanksgiving).
- Christmas: hot orders close noon Mon 12/21. Hot delivery Dec 22 to 24.
- After the hot cutoff, cold only: last day to order is Wed 12/23 (the day before pickup). Pickup Christmas Eve, through 5pm.
- Christmas Day: closed online, special requests case by case. Never offer it as an ordering option.
- Hanukkah (first night Fri 12/4) and New Year's Eve (Thu 12/31): 48 hours' notice, hot or cold.
- Corporate track sells office holiday parties, 48 hours' notice.
- Resends to non-openers are manual next-day "did not open" segments (Mailchimp Essentials).
- Nothing gets scheduled until Tro approves the test emails.
- Copy style: direct, plain, no em-dashes.

## Status when paused (Sat 10/10)
- Emails generated by build.py. Layout QA'd with stand-in images at 640, 375 and 320px: tiles and buttons line up, no overflow. Recheck once real photos are in.
- Product photos come from the store's Vercel blob (see build.py keys). The cloud sandbox couldn't download them.
  On a normal computer: download them, center-crop with `_images.json` (hero 1200x720, tile 520x372, sq 300x300, JPEG ~q76),
  commit to `email/holiday/` in this repo, and point IMGBASE at the jsDelivr URL pinned to that commit.
- One test file (holiday26-spinach-tile.jpg) may be in Mailchimp Content Studio; it can be deleted.

## Files
- `holiday/photos.json`: image key to store photo URL.
- `holiday/_images.json`: crops each image key needs. Name files `<key>-<crop>.jpg` (crop = hero, tile or sq).
- build.py also expects `logo.png` under IMGBASE (copy `email/logo-2749a8c85b.png` to `email/holiday/logo.png`).
- Send dates were updated after Tro's last note: Home starts Mon 11/30 (not Sun 11/29).
